Who we are

James Talbot Woodworking is a boutique fine-craftsmanship studio dedicated to creating bespoke cabinetry and furniture that blend technical precision with artistic sensibility.

Every project begins with listening—understanding how you live, work, and use your space. From there, we design and build pieces that are as functional as they are beautiful.

Whether it's a full kitchen remodel, a single statement piece, or a complete 3D visualization of your vision, we bring the same care and attention to every job.

Based locally, we work closely with homeowners, designers, and builders to deliver work that stands the test of time.
